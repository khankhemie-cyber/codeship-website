# Explorers Teacher Manual
**Document ID:** CSA-EXP-TM-EN-v1.0 · **Program:** Explorers (K–Grade 1) · Companion to CSA-EXP-MCM-EN

---

## How to use this volume

One spread per session: objectives and materials on top, the minute-by-minute plan beneath, callouts where you need them. Timing is the four-phase model (Warm-up 5 → Teach 10 → Build 30 → Reflect 10); on quiz days (x.3, x.5) the first 10 minutes are the quiz, then a 5-minute re-teach. Workbook page and printable references point to CSA-EXP-SW-EN and CSA-EXP-PRB-EN. 💻 marks online-delivery adaptations.

**Preparation checklist (every session):** room/tech per CSA-STD-OPS §2 · block-coding app fresh on every device · today's printable copied · workbooks out · visual schedule posted · fallback unplugged activity packed.

> **📌 Teacher Note (program-wide):** With K–1, narrate everything you demonstrate ("I'm dragging the *move right* block…"), keep Teach truly to 10 minutes, and get devices into hands fast. Attention is won in the Build.

## Semester 1 — My Helpful Robot

### S1.1 Be the Robot
**Objectives:** give/follow step-by-step instructions; state that computers do exactly what they're told, in order.
**Materials:** direction arrow cards (PRB-1), painter's tape floor grid, workbook p. S1.1. Devices optional (last 10 min of Build only).

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–5 | Warm-up | Names + one thing you love to make. Introduce the visual schedule. |
| 5–15 | Teach | **You are the robot.** Instructor follows children's literal instructions to walk to the door ("step, step, turn…"). When they say "go to the door," don't move — *robots need every step.* |
| 15–45 | Build | Pairs: one "programmer" uses arrow cards, one "robot" walks the tape grid to a goal. Swap. **Diagnostic:** observe with the First Voyage checklist (AM §3). Last 10 min: show the app on the big screen — "next week these arrows become blocks." |
| 45–55 | Reflect | Circle: "What happens if the steps are in the wrong order?" Workbook: draw yourself as a robot. Goal sticker. |

> **🗣 Suggested Dialogue:** "A computer is very fast and very obedient — but it can't guess. It only does exactly what we tell it, in exactly that order."
> **🤝 Every Learner:** children who don't want to be watched walking the grid can drive a toy or finger-puppet along a desk grid instead.
> 💻 Online: children command a *parent or stuffed animal* robot at home; instructor models on camera.

### S1.2 First Blocks
**Objectives:** connect blocks into a program that moves a character; predict what a sequence will do.
**Materials:** devices with app; predict-the-path printable (PRB-2); workbook p. S1.2.

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–5 | Warm-up | Flash 3 arrow cards; children air-trace the path the robot would walk. |
| 5–15 | Teach | Big screen: open the app, name the parts (stage, character, blocks). Drag *start* + two *move* blocks; **ask for a prediction before every run.** Snap blocks together like train cars. |
| 15–45 | Build | Children build their first program: make the character reach the star (pre-set scene). Core: 3-block sequence. Lighter: 2 blocks, larger target. Stretch: add a jump or sound block. |
| 45–55 | Reflect | Partner shows their program running. Workbook: circle the blocks you used. |

> **⚠️ Watch For:** blocks not snapped (program stops early) — teach "check the train is connected" as the first debugging habit.

### S1.3 Which Way? (Quiz Q1)
**Objectives:** navigate a character up/down/left/right to a goal; describe a path with direction words.
**Materials:** Q1 picture quiz (AM); grid maze printable (PRB-3); devices.

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–10 | Quiz Q1 | Picture quiz, read aloud item by item (AM §Q1). Collect with a cheer — "quizzes help *me* teach better." |
| 10–15 | Re-teach | Revisit the item most missed, concretely, with arrow cards. |
| 15–45 | Build | Maze scenes: program the character through the maze to the goal. Core: one maze. Lighter: straight-line maze. Stretch: maze with a detour ("visit the apple first"). |
| 45–55 | Reflect | Children give a partner spoken directions ("up, up, right…") to retrace their route. Workbook path-tracing page. |

### S1.4 Make It Start
**Objectives:** use start and tap triggers; explain "when this happens, that starts."
**Materials:** devices; trigger cards (PRB-4).

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–5 | Warm-up | Simon-says with triggers: "*When* I clap, jump. *When* I touch my nose, sit." |
| 5–15 | Teach | Big screen: same dance, in code — green-flag trigger vs. tap trigger on a character. Tap the character: it spins. "The tap is the *event*. The spin is what starts." |
| 15–45 | Build | Two characters: one starts on green flag, one starts on tap. Core: both triggers used. Lighter: one trigger. Stretch: character that speaks when tapped. Begin planning the Helpful Robot (workbook planner: *Who does my robot help? What job does it do?*). |
| 45–55 | Reflect | "Show me your tap surprise." Goal: what will your robot do next week? |

### S1.5 My Helpful Robot — build & share (Quiz Q2)
**Objectives:** combine sequence + trigger into the semester project; present to a partner.
**Materials:** Q2 quiz (AM); devices; project rubric (AM §8); Home Link note (PRB-5).

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–10 | Quiz Q2 | Picture quiz on sequencing + triggers. |
| 10–15 | Re-teach | Fix-the-order demo from quiz results. |
| 15–45 | Build | Finish Helpful Robot: robot character does its helpful job (ordered moves) with at least one trigger. Instructor scores rubric observations during circulation. |
| 45–55 | Reflect | Partner presentations with the frame: "My robot helps ___ by ___." Partners give one "I like…". Home Link note goes home. |

> **📌 Teacher Note:** rubric evidence can be *spoken* — "tell me what each block does" counts fully for Concept & Technical criteria (CSA-STD-CF §7).

## Semester 2 — Kindness Cards

### S2.1 Kind Words, Big Plans
**Objectives:** identify kind vs. unkind messages; plan a card for a real person.
**Materials:** kind/unkind sorting pictures (PRB-6); card planner (workbook p. S2.1).

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–5 | Warm-up | Retrieve: "Show with your arms how blocks snap in order." |
| 5–15 | Teach | Story moment: a message can make someone's whole day. Sort picture-messages into 😊 / 😟 piles as a class. "This semester we *build* kindness — a card that moves, talks, and surprises." |
| 15–45 | Build | Planner: who is my card for, what will it say/show, what happens when they tap it. Draw the card design. Early finishers open the app and choose characters/backgrounds. |
| 45–55 | Reflect | Share plans in circle. "Kind words we could use" anchor chart. |

### S2.2 When You Tap…
**Objectives:** build a tap-triggered if-then interaction.
**Materials:** devices; planners from S2.1.

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–5 | Warm-up | Trigger Simon-says (from S1.4) — now children call the "when… do…" rules. |
| 5–15 | Teach | Big screen: **IF you tap the heart, THEN it grows and giggles.** Say the words *if* and *then* every time. Build one interaction live from a child's suggestion. |
| 15–45 | Build | Children build their card's main scene with one tap → response. Core: one if-then. Lighter: pre-placed character, child adds the response. Stretch: two different tappable things. |
| 45–55 | Reflect | Gallery walk: tap a neighbour's card. Workbook: draw your if → then with arrows. |

### S2.3 Sounds & Surprises (Quiz Q3)
**Objectives:** record a kind voice message; understand that shared things can be seen by others.
**Materials:** Q3 quiz; devices with mic access.

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–10 | Quiz Q3 | Picture quiz on if-then + kindness scenarios. |
| 10–15 | Re-teach | Act out the most-missed if-then with two volunteers. |
| 15–45 | Build | Record a kind message into the card (quiet corner rotation for recording). While waiting: polish scenes. **Mini-lesson during rotation:** "When we share something online, other people can see it — so we share kind things and keep private things private." |
| 45–55 | Reflect | Play one classmate's recording (with permission). "How did it feel to hear it?" |

> **⚠️ Watch For:** recording chaos — one recording station, headphones if available, visual queue cards.

### S2.4 My Digital Footprint
**Objectives:** sort safe-to-share vs. keep-private; finish building cards.
**Materials:** footprint sorting cards (PRB-7); devices.

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–5 | Warm-up | "Tell your partner your card's if → then." |
| 5–15 | Teach | Giant footprint poster: everything we share online leaves a footprint. Sort cards *onto* the footprint (first name, my drawing — OK) or *into the lockbox* (full name, address, school name — private). |
| 15–45 | Build | Finish cards: message + if-then + recording. Check: does my card share anything private? Fix if so. |
| 45–55 | Reflect | Workbook footprint page: circle what's safe to share. |

### S2.5 Card Delivery Day (Quiz Q4)
**Objectives:** present and deliver the card; give warm feedback.
**Materials:** Q4 quiz; devices; rubric; Home Link (PRB-5 variant).

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–10 | Quiz Q4 | Semester 2 quiz (if-then, footprint). |
| 10–15 | Re-teach | Footprint lockbox rapid re-sort. |
| 15–45 | Build | Final polish; rehearse with the frame: "My card is for ___. When you tap ___, ___." Instructor scores rubric. |
| 45–55 | Reflect | Deliveries/presentations; each presenter gets one "I like…" from the class. Cards go home (or are shared with recipients per consent rules). |

## Semester 3 — Recycling Sorter

### S3.1 Sort It Out
**Objectives:** sort real objects by rule; state rules as if-then.
**Materials:** sorting bin objects (clean recyclables + non), rule cards (PRB-8).

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–5 | Warm-up | "What does *if… then…* mean? Show me with your arms." |
| 5–15 | Teach | Dump the mystery bin. Sort together: **IF it's paper THEN blue bin.** Children chant the rule for each item. "A program can make choices with the same rules." |
| 15–45 | Build | Stations: physical sorting races by rule; then app — open the sorter starter scene, meet the characters (items + bins). Plan: which items will my sorter know? |
| 45–55 | Reflect | "Tell me a sorting rule from your house." Workbook rule page. |

### S3.2 Teaching the Sorter
**Objectives:** build tap-responses so different items respond differently.
**Materials:** devices.

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–5 | Warm-up | Item flash cards → children point to the right bin corner of the room. |
| 5–15 | Teach | Big screen: tap the banana peel → it hops to the green bin and says "compost!" Tap the bottle → blue bin. Two rules, two behaviours. |
| 15–45 | Build | Children program 2 items with different tap responses. Core: 2 items. Lighter: 1 item, pre-built second. Stretch: item that goes to a *wrong* bin and says "oops — try again!" |
| 45–55 | Reflect | Pair-test each other's sorters. Goal setting. |

### S3.3 Bug Hunt (Quiz Q5)
**Objectives:** find and fix a planted bug; define a bug as a fixable mistake.
**Materials:** Q5 quiz; "buggy" demo project pre-loaded; bug-hunt badge stickers (PRB-9).

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–10 | Quiz Q5 | Picture quiz on sorting rules/if-then. |
| 10–15 | Re-teach | Re-sort the most-missed item together. |
| 15–45 | Build | **Bug Hunt:** instructor's sorter is broken (paper goes to the wrong bin; one item does nothing). Children hunt in pairs on the demo copy: What did we *want*? What *happened*? Which block is wrong? Fix it. Then: test your own sorter and fix anything odd. Badge sticker per fixed bug. |
| 45–55 | Reflect | "A bug is a mistake we can FIND and FIX." Circle-share one bug found. |

> **🗣 Suggested Dialogue:** "Real programmers find bugs every single day. Finding a bug doesn't mean you did it wrong — it means you're checking like a pro."

### S3.4 Test and Fix
**Objectives:** self-test, debug own project, extend to a third category.
**Materials:** devices; test checklist card (PRB-10).

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–5 | Warm-up | "What are the 3 bug-hunter questions?" (want / happened / which block) |
| 5–15 | Teach | Model using the test checklist on my own project: tap every item, tick or fix. |
| 15–45 | Build | Children run the checklist, fix bugs, then add a third item or bin. Core: all items tested + one new item. Stretch: a "tricky" item (juice box!) with a spoken explanation. |
| 45–55 | Reflect | Trade devices: partner tries to "break" your sorter kindly; note anything found. |

### S3.5 Sorter Demo Day (Quiz Q6)
**Objectives:** demonstrate the sorter; explain one fixed bug.
**Materials:** Q6 quiz; rubric; Home Link.

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–10 | Quiz Q6 | Semester quiz (if-then, debugging). |
| 10–15 | Re-teach | Fastest-fingers bug spot on the big screen. |
| 15–45 | Build | Final fixes; rehearse demo: "My sorter knows ___ items. My favourite bug I fixed was ___." Rubric scoring. |
| 45–55 | Reflect | Demos + "I like… / I wonder…" (introduce "I wonder…" here — it's the gentle version of a question). |

## Semester 4 — My Neighbourhood Map

### S4.1 Places I Know
**Objectives:** draw a 3-place map; plan the app scene.
**Materials:** paper, map planner (workbook p. S4.1); example maps.

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–5 | Warm-up | "Point the direction of the door… the window… your home (guess!)." |
| 5–15 | Teach | Show a simple picture map. "Code can show *real* places. This semester your app is a map of YOUR world — home, school, park, grandma's, anywhere you know." |
| 15–45 | Build | Draw the paper map (3 places + paths). Choose app background and characters that match. Lighter: 2 places. Stretch: 4+ places with a legend. |
| 45–55 | Reflect | Map museums: walk and look. "Which place will your character visit first?" |

### S4.2 Walking the Map
**Objectives:** program an ordered route between places.
**Materials:** devices; paper maps beside devices.

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–5 | Warm-up | Arrow-card path retrace from S1 — "we knew this in Semester 1!" |
| 5–15 | Teach | Big screen: set the scene, place the character at "home," program the route to "school" (move blocks in order). Predict-then-run. |
| 15–45 | Build | Program route between first two places. Core: one full route. Lighter: shorter route. Stretch: round trip, or a second character with its own route. |
| 45–55 | Reflect | Show a partner; partner guesses where the character is going before it runs. |

### S4.3 Tap the Places (Quiz Q7)
**Objectives:** add tap triggers to map locations.
**Materials:** Q7 quiz; devices.

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–10 | Quiz Q7 | Picture quiz (direction, sequencing). |
| 10–15 | Re-teach | Direction confusion fix with floor grid. |
| 15–45 | Build | Make places tappable: tap the park → birds sing; tap school → character says "I learn here!" Core: 2 tap triggers. Stretch: recorded voice labels for places. |
| 45–55 | Reflect | Workbook: draw your favourite tap surprise. |

### S4.4 Fix the Journey
**Objectives:** debug routes; enrich the scene.
**Materials:** devices; test checklist (PRB-10).

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–5 | Warm-up | Bug-hunter questions chant. |
| 5–15 | Teach | Model a route bug: character walks *through* the pond. Debug aloud with the 3 questions. |
| 15–45 | Build | Test-and-fix all routes and taps with checklist; add one detail (weather, pet, sound). Rehearse tour sentences. |
| 45–55 | Reflect | Partner swap-test. Goal: what to polish before Tour Day. |

### S4.5 Map Tour Day (Quiz Q8)
**Objectives:** present a guided tour of the map.
**Materials:** Q8 quiz; rubric; Home Link + capstone note home.

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–10 | Quiz Q8 | Semester quiz (integration). |
| 10–15 | Re-teach | Class-choice replay of any tricky idea. |
| 15–45 | Build | Final polish; tour rehearsal: "Welcome to my neighbourhood! This is ___. Watch what happens when I tap ___." Rubric scoring. |
| 45–55 | Reflect | Tours to the class; warm feedback; capstone teaser: "Next time YOU choose what we build." |

## Capstone — Show-and-Tell App

### C.1 My Own Idea
**Objectives:** choose an own idea; build the main scene.
**Materials:** capstone picture planner (PRB-11); devices; idea-spark cards for the stuck.

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–5 | Warm-up | Year-in-blocks: hold up a block card, class shouts what it does. |
| 5–15 | Teach | "You've built a robot, a card, a sorter, and a map. Today: anything you want." Planner: *My app is about… When you tap… It moves like…* Show 2 past examples ONLY as sparks, then hide them. |
| 15–45 | Build | Plan (10 min max) then build. Requirement check on planner: a sequence AND a trigger. Instructor circulates with "What do you want it to do?" coaching only. |
| 45–55 | Reflect | Stand-up: one sentence — "My app is about ___." |

### C.2 Showcase
**Objectives:** finish, rehearse, present to families.
**Materials:** devices; presentation frame cards (PRB-12); certificates (AM §9); rubric.

| Time | Phase | Plan |
|------|-------|------|
| 0–5 | Warm-up | Calm setup; rehearse frame silently with cards. |
| 5–15 | Teach | Presentation mini-lesson: loud voice, show the tap, say what you're proud of. Model a 3-sentence presentation. |
| 15–45 | Build | Finish + rehearse in pairs; instructor scores capstone rubric; showcase stations set. |
| 45–55 | Reflect | **Family showcase:** each Explorer presents "My app is… When you tap… I'm proud of…". Certificates presented. |

> **🏠 Home Link:** families attend C.2 (in person or on the call 💻). Send the showcase invitation two weeks ahead (PH §7).

## Troubleshooting quick table

| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| Program "does nothing" | No start trigger, or blocks not snapped | Check the flag/tap block; check the train is connected |
| Character walks the wrong way | Left/right confusion | Floor-grid re-walk; arrow stickers on device edges |
| Child says "I can't do it" | Overwhelm, not ability | Shrink task to lighter-load variant; sit beside, "you point, I drag," hand back control fast |
| Recording won't play | Mic permission or volume | Instructor-only settings fix; never let students into settings |
| Tablet frozen | App overload | Swap for spare (OPS §3), restart quietly later |

## Homework guidance

Explorers have **no required homework**. Home Links suggest optional 10-minute family activities (be-the-robot at dinner, spot bins that sort, draw maps). Never send device-dependent homework at this age.

## Parent talking points (pickup)

- "Ask them to *be your robot* on the way home — exact steps only!"
- "Ask: what's a bug? You'll love the answer."
- "They presented today and nailed their three sentences."

## Closure script (every session)

"Explorers, hands on your head when your work is saved… Our goal for next time is on the board. You dreamed it, you built it — see you next week. Dream. Code. Achieve!"
