# Explorers Parent Handbook
**Document ID:** CSA-EXP-PH-EN-v1.0 · **Program:** Explorers (K–Grade 1) · Given to every family at enrolment.

---

## 1. Welcome to the CODEship Journey

Your child is an **Explorer** — the first level of the CODEship Journey (Dream → Code → Achieve) that runs from Kindergarten to Grade 8. Explorers learn computational thinking through visual block coding: sequencing, triggers, and simple logic, alongside early lessons in kindness online.

By the end of the program, your Explorer **gives a computer clear instructions and builds a simple interactive app** — presented to you at a family showcase.

Our promise to every family: *Creativity Before Code.* Children bring ideas; we provide tools, structure, and encouragement. Every project is genuinely theirs.

## 2. How the program works

- **4 semesters + a capstone**, about 20 sessions plus the capstone, each **55 minutes**.
- Every session: **Warm-up → Teach → Build → Reflect** — most of class is hands-on building.
- **One real project per semester**, then a capstone app of your child's own design.
- **2 short quizzes per semester (8 total)** — read aloud, picture-based, low-stakes; they help us teach, they never rank children.
- Inclusive-design accommodations are built into every class. Tell us what your child needs and we'll plan together before the first session.

## 3. Semester roadmap

| Semester | Project | Your child learns |
|----------|---------|-------------------|
| 1 | My Helpful Robot | Sequencing, triggers & events, position & direction |
| 2 | Kindness Cards | Simple if-then logic, kindness online & digital footprint |
| 3 | Recycling Sorter | If-then logic, debugging |
| 4 | My Neighbourhood Map | Position & direction, sequencing, debugging |
| ★ | Show-and-Tell App (capstone) | Everything combined — their own idea, presented to you |

## 4. What you'll hear from us

- A **Home Link note** at each semester's start and end (what we're building + a 10-minute family activity).
- After each semester project: **rubric results plus 2–3 sentences of warm, specific feedback** about your child's growth.
- Quiz days are celebrated, never graded publicly.
- A **showcase invitation** about two weeks before the capstone.

## 5. Technology at home

**None required.** Explorers has no required homework and no home software. If you'd like to extend learning, the free activities in our Home Link notes need only your kitchen table. If your child wants to keep building, a free visual block-coding app (we use a ScratchJr-style environment in class) on a family tablet is plenty — 15–20 minutes at a time is right for this age.

Online-class families need: a tablet or computer, a quiet-ish spot, and a grown-up nearby for the first 10 minutes. We'll help you test the setup before session one.

## 6. Questions to ask after class

Instead of "what did you learn?", try:

- "Can you *be my robot*? I'll give you instructions." (Semester 1 — expect delighted literalism.)
- "What happens when you tap it?" (Semester 2)
- "What's a *bug*? Did you fix one today?" (Semester 3 — bugs are good news here.)
- "Take me on a tour of your map." (Semester 4)
- Always safe: "What are you proud of building?"

## 7. Progress and showcase expectations

Children this age grow in bursts. What steady progress looks like: naming what their blocks do → predicting what a program will do → fixing their own bugs → presenting three confident sentences. The capstone showcase (final session) is a family event — your Explorer presents their own app live. Certificates are presented there.

## 8. Our classroom values

We are kind · We try, test, and try again · We take care of the equipment · We share the room. Screens at CODEship are for **making, not watching** — your child is the author, not the audience.

## 9. Practical notes

Drop-off/pickup at the room door with roster check (in person); children are released only to listed adults. Online classes admit children from the waiting room by roster name. Questions or accommodation updates any time: speak with your instructor at pickup or contact your location lead.
