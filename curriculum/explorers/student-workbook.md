# Explorers Student Workbook
**Document ID:** CSA-EXP-SW-EN-v1.0 · **Program:** Explorers (K–Grade 1) · Print spec: 12 pt+ body, icon-first, heavy line-work for pre-readers, perfect-bound with perforated certificate page.

*Designer note: every page pairs a small adult-readable caption with big icons and drawing space. Children “read” this book by pictures. One workbook page (or spread) per session, plus trackers front and back.*

---

## Front matter

### Welcome aboard! (p. 1)
Illustration of the CODEship with an empty porthole frame: **Draw yourself on the ship!** Name line with handwriting guides. Caption: *You are an Explorer. This year you will teach a computer to follow YOUR ideas.*

### Our class agreements (p. 2)
Four icon panels to colour as each is learned: We are kind · We try, test, and try again · We take care of the equipment · We share the room. (→ CSA-STD-OPS §5)

### My goal tracker (p. 3)
A winding path of 22 circles (one per session) from harbour to lighthouse. Child adds a sticker or colours a circle each session. Quiz sessions marked with a small flag; project days with a star; showcase with fireworks.

## Semester 1 — My Helpful Robot 🤖

**Semester opener (p. 4):** Big Idea in adult-caption + picture: *Computers follow clear, ordered instructions.* Colour-in robot.

| Page | Session | Activity |
|------|---------|----------|
| S1.1 | Be the Robot | Draw yourself as a robot. Circle the pictures that are “exact steps” vs. “guessing.” |
| S1.2 | First Blocks | Trace the block train. Circle the blocks YOU used today. Predict: draw where the cat lands after 3 arrow moves. |
| S1.3 | Which Way? | Maze page: trace the path with a crayon, then draw the arrow cards in order. Quiz-day flag sticker spot. |
| S1.4 | Make It Start | Match trigger to action with lines: 🚩→run, 👆→spin. **My robot plan:** Who does my robot help? (draw) What job does it do? (draw) |
| S1.5 | Project day | **My Helpful Robot** frame: draw your finished robot scene. Vocabulary review icons: sequence, trigger, direction. My reflection: circle the face (😀🙂😐) for “I can put steps in order.” |

**Knowledge-check pages:** small “I can” self-checks after each quiz — *I can put pictures in order (Q1). I can make things start with a tap (Q2).*

## Semester 2 — Kindness Cards 💛

**Semester opener (p. 10):** *What we build and share online can make someone's day better.* Colour the hearts.

| Page | Session | Activity |
|------|---------|----------|
| S2.1 | Kind Words, Big Plans | **Card planner:** My card is for ___ (draw them). It will show ___ . Kind words bank with icons to circle. |
| S2.2 | When You Tap… | Draw your IF (finger tapping) → THEN (what happens) with the big arrow. |
| S2.3 | Sounds & Surprises | Draw the sound waves of your recorded message; circle who may hear shared things (just me / my class / more people — guided). |
| S2.4 | My Digital Footprint | Footprint page: stick or draw SAFE-to-share things on the footprint; PRIVATE things in the lockbox. |
| S2.5 | Delivery day | Draw the person receiving your card. Reflection faces: “My card was kind.” “I kept private things private.” |

## Semester 3 — Recycling Sorter ♻️

**Semester opener (p. 16):** *Programs can help us make good decisions.* Colour the three bins.

| Page | Session | Activity |
|------|---------|----------|
| S3.1 | Sort It Out | Cut-and-sort (or draw lines): items → correct bins. Say the rule: IF ___ THEN ___ (icon frame). |
| S3.2 | Teaching the Sorter | My sorter knows these items (draw 2–3). Match item → bin → what it says. |
| S3.3 | Bug Hunt | **Bug-hunter badge page:** draw the bug you found. The 3 questions in icons: Want? Happened? Which block? |
| S3.4 | Test and Fix | Test checklist with tick boxes (icon rows): I tapped every item ☐ Everything worked ☐ I fixed ___ bugs (write the number!). |
| S3.5 | Demo day | Draw your sorter working. Reflection: “Bugs are mistakes we can fix” — colour the bug being fixed. |

## Semester 4 — My Neighbourhood Map 🗺️

**Semester opener (p. 22):** *Code can represent the real world around us.*

| Page | Session | Activity |
|------|---------|----------|
| S4.1 | Places I Know | Big map frame: draw 3 real places and the paths between them. |
| S4.2 | Walking the Map | Draw your character's route with arrows in order (1st, 2nd, 3rd stickers). |
| S4.3 | Tap the Places | Draw your favourite tap surprise. Which place? What happens? |
| S4.4 | Fix the Journey | Checklist page (like S3.4) + draw one detail you added. |
| S4.5 | Tour day | **My tour card:** Welcome to my neighbourhood! This is ___ . Watch when I tap ___ . Reflection faces for the whole semester. |

## Capstone — Show-and-Tell App ⭐

| Page | Session | Activity |
|------|---------|----------|
| C.1 | My Own Idea | **Capstone planner (double spread):** My app is about ___ (draw big!). When you tap ___ → ___ . My character moves like this (arrows). Requirement check icons: sequence ☐ trigger ☐. |
| C.2 | Showcase | **My presentation card:** My app is… / When you tap… / I'm proud of… (picture prompts). Autograph page for classmates. |

## Back matter

### My vocabulary treasure chest (p. 30)
Icon glossary the child colours as each word is learned: instruction · block · sequence · direction · trigger · event · if/then · share · private · footprint · sort · rule · bug · debug · test · map · route.

### Semester reflection pages (after each semester)
Same three prompts each time, picture-supported: *The best thing I built… The trickiest part… Next I want to make…* (draw + optional words).

### My progress & achievement tracker (p. 31)
Grid of the 6 program skills with 4 star-slots each; instructor or child stars them as they grow. Bug-hunter badges, presenter badges, kindness badge sticker spots.

### Notes & doodles (p. 32)
Free page. Caption: *Great builders doodle their ideas.*

### Certificate (perforated, p. 33)
**CODEship Academy — Explorer's Certificate of Achievement.** “___ gave a computer clear instructions and built their very own app.” Lines for date, instructor signature, location. Brand navy/gold frame. (Issue criteria → CSA-EXP-AM-EN §9.)
