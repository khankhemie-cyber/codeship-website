# Explorers Printable Resource Book
**Document ID:** CSA-EXP-PRB-EN-v1.0 · **Program:** Explorers (K–Grade 1) · Single-sided photocopy masters, black-and-white safe (→ CSA-STD-PUB §11).

Each master below is one printable page. PRB numbers are referenced from the Teacher Manual.

---

## PRB-1 — Direction Arrow Cards (S1.1)
Cut-apart deck: ↑ ↓ ← → plus START and STOP cards, palm-sized, thick borders. 2 sets per page. Also used S1.3, S4.2 warm-ups.

## PRB-2 — Predict the Path (S1.2)
Worksheet: three short block-sequences (pictured as snapped trains); child draws where the character ends on a 4×4 picture grid. Answer strip along the bottom for instructor, fold-back before copying.

## PRB-3 — Grid Mazes (S1.3)
Three mazes, rising difficulty: straight line / one turn / detour-via-apple. Grid squares match the arrow cards' vocabulary.

## PRB-4 — Trigger Cards (S1.4)
Matching deck: WHEN cards (🚩 flag, 👆 tap, 💥 bump) and DO cards (run, spin, speak, grow). Used for Simon-says and matching games; reused S2.2.

## PRB-5 — Home Link Notes (Sx.5, one variant per semester)
Half-page notes home. Fixed layout: *What we built · Ask your Explorer · Try at home (10-minute, no-device family activity)*. Variants: S1 be-the-robot at dinner; S2 make a paper kindness card together; S3 spot your home's sorting rules; S4 walk your real map route.

## PRB-6 — Kind or Not? Sorting Pictures (S2.1)
Twelve picture-message cards (smiling notes, sharing, teasing, excluding) for 😊/😟 class sorting. No text beyond names.

## PRB-7 — Footprint Sort (S2.4)
Giant footprint poster (2-page tape-together) + lockbox picture + 10 item cards: first name, my drawing, my art, favourite colour (safe) / full name, address, school name, phone number, photo of my house, password (private). Also the template for the workbook footprint page.

## PRB-8 — Sorting Rule Cards (S3.1)
IF/THEN sentence-frame cards with picture slots: IF [item picture] THEN [bin picture]. Blank frames included for children's own rules.

## PRB-9 — Bug-Hunter Badges (S3.3)
Sticker-sized badge circles: magnifying-glass bug logo, "BUG FOUND & FIXED". 20 per page. Award liberally.

## PRB-10 — Test Checklist Card (S3.4, S4.4)
Icon checklist card: 👆 tap every item ☐ · 👀 watch what happens ☐ · ❓ want vs. happened ☐ · 🔧 fix and test again ☐. Print on card stock, one per child, reused across semesters.

## PRB-11 — Capstone Picture Planner (C.1)
Full page, 4 panels: *My app is about (draw big)* · *When you tap ___ → ___* · *My character moves (arrow strip)* · requirement check: sequence ☐ trigger ☐.

## PRB-12 — Presentation Frame Cards (C.2)
Palm cards with picture prompts: "My app is…" / "When you tap…" / "I'm proud of…". Also the S1.5/S2.5/S3.5/S4.5 frames in mini form on one page.

## PRB-13 — Visual Schedule Poster
Warm-up → Teach → Build → Reflect as four icon panels with clock faces (5/10/30/10). Posted every session (→ CSA-STD-OPS §2).

## PRB-14 — Class Agreements Poster
The four agreements as icon panels (→ CSA-STD-OPS §5), colour-in version for S1.1.

## PRB-15 — Goal & Progress Tracker (classroom wall)
Harbour-to-lighthouse path matching the workbook tracker, poster-sized, one boat marker per child (first names only).

## PRB-16 — Unplugged Fallback Pack
Tech-failure standbys, one per semester: giant floor-grid robot game (S1) · paper if-then flap cards (S2) · physical sorting relay (S3) · classroom treasure map with arrow-card routes (S4).

## PRB-17 — Certificate Master
The Explorer's Certificate (→ SW back matter, AM §9 criteria) in photocopy-safe line-art with navy/gold print version.
